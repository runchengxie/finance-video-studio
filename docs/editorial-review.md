# Video manuscript editorial review

Apply this project brief when drafting, reviewing or revising video manuscripts, for both the v1 short format and full v2 projects. Review evidence before polishing the prose, then inspect the finished draft again. Select 简体中文 in the documentation language menu for the reusable Chinese prompt, also available in the [source document](https://github.com/runchengxie/finance-video-studio/blob/main/docs/editorial-review.zh.md).

Supply the task, intended audience, draft and available sources together. Keep real manuscripts and source records in an external task directory. This page records project requirements. It bundles no third-party skill files and starts no model or checker automatically.

## Reusable brief

Evaluate the user's premises and manuscript independently. Identify factual errors, biased framing and material omissions directly, with evidence and their effect on the conclusion. Praise and emotional reassurance are unnecessary. Retain supported claims, and require evidence for objections too.

Examine the question before answering it. Check whether the title presupposes success or failure, whether the comparator is appropriate, and whether the reader is being promised an answer the evidence cannot supply. Verify denominators, periods and the distinction between observation and inference. Narrow claims or gather material when support is insufficient.

Use academic and practitioner perspectives when they materially improve the review. Academic questions may concern causal identification, sampling or external validity. Practitioners may question costs, execution constraints or actual user behavior. Consider other relevant roles without mechanically adding every viewpoint. Label these as the reviewer's analytical questions unless real experts were consulted. Never invent interviews, credentials, endorsement or consensus.

Seek the strongest relevant counterevidence. Check for selective examples, state what would change the conclusion, and incorporate applicable favorable findings within their actual scope. Repeated caution cannot substitute for analysis.

Prefer original sources. Record publication or update dates, version dates, data coverage and retrieval dates separately. Mark unknown dates explicitly. A retrieval timestamp cannot establish publication time, and a DOI or URL year cannot establish a paper's publication year. Distinguish working papers from published versions and identify revisions to estimates or comparison groups.

Discuss whether older evidence still applies at the writing date. Recheck changeable rules, fees and product arrangements before publication. Explain institutional or market differences before transferring findings. Record whether the full source, abstract or search excerpt was read. Disclose access failures and unverified clauses without claiming full-document verification.

State judgments directly with their evidence. Avoid inventing a reader misconception and overturning it for rhetorical effect, including equivalent contrast formulas. Avoid three or more mechanically parallel clauses, concrete poetic actions assigned to abstract nouns, and conclusions inflated to civilization or everyone. Stop when the material has finished its work.

Lead sentences with the actor and action, then add time, cause and conditions. Rewrite Chinese sentences crowded with four occurrences of 的. Prefer concrete verbs and supported outcomes over nominalized claims of improvement or growth. Remove unnecessary conjunctions while retaining necessary causal and conditional meaning.

Vary sentence length naturally. Do not end every paragraph with a short verdict or explain emotion already conveyed by an action or sourced quotation. Repeat necessary terms instead of forcing synonyms. Write for listening, reducing spoken numeric load while preserving complete precision in visuals and source records.

For Chinese manuscripts, use Chinese punctuation and full-width parentheses. Do not use dash punctuation. Colons introduce directly sourced speech only. Minimize double quotation marks, semicolons and Markdown bold. Preserve required syntax in URLs, code and machine fields. Remove stock insight announcements and inflated business jargon specified in the Chinese prompt.

After drafting, rewrite passages that sound like task completion, remove or narrow claims larger than the evidence, and stop at the natural endpoint. Rule checkers supplement human inspection of meaning, facts and rhythm.

## Retained review record

Save a separate revised draft and preserve the original. Keep a short companion describing premise checks, material evidence gaps, treatment of counterevidence and actual changes to judgments. Explain the contribution of specialist perspectives when used, without a decorative role list.

The source record contains source identity, link, publication/version date, coverage period, retrieval date, access scope and current applicability. Unknowns remain explicit. Shorten unsupported drafts rather than inventing material to meet a length target.

Save actual checker output when executed. Do not report checks, reading aloud or listening as completed unless performed. A checker pass does not establish semantic or factual validity.

This is an editorial instruction, not an automatically executed CLI check or a new model-scored approval state. Changed content still requires a new review revision under the [production review workflow](production-rendering.md), and rendering retains its existing exact-version human approval requirement.
